A note from the director

Hi, I’m Sheina.

I’m a junior at UC Berkeley studying Data Science and Business at Haas, and my work sits at the intersection of AI, product, and strategy.

This past summer that meant building an agentic procurement platform at Cloudflare. Before that it meant launching Perplexity on campus and supporting M&A deals at Deloitte. In high school I founded a cement company that rebuilt earthquake-damaged infrastructure and ended up on CNN. I’m still not entirely sure how that happened.

What connects all of it is a tendency to show up at the messy early stage of hard problems, figure out what is actually going on, and build something that makes it better.

Lately I’ve been doing more of the building myself as I move toward AI/ML. This little picture house is where I keep what I make along the way. The first two started with things that bugged me: the air I grew up breathing, and how many cookies follow me around the internet.

Warmly,Sheina

P.S. If you also boulder or have a movie I need to watch, tell me. Both require more patience than people expect.

Feature No. 1Live data

Air Quality Explorer

  • web app
  • 2026
  • data viz
  • forecasting
  • maps

Live PM2.5 for 25 cities, mostly around the Bay plus big U.S. cities and Jakarta. Click a city for a week of history, a five-day forecast, a plain-English health note, and a small model that picks the cleanest hour to head outside. It grew out of my PM2.5 research at the Energy Institute at Haas.

A note on the print: data from a global air-quality model, so it shows regional air, not street-level sensors.

Feature No. 2v1.0.0

Cookie Lens

  • chrome extension
  • 2026
  • privacy
  • interface design

A Chrome extension that shows every cookie on the page you’re on and labels the trackers with a plain reason, like “set by pubmatic.com, a known ad tracker, lasts 3 months.” Delete them one at a time or all at once, and open the dashboard to see which companies follow you across the most sites.

A note on the print: ratings come from an open cookie database and Disconnect’s tracker list. Nothing leaves your browser.

about me

Hi, I’m Sheina.

I’m Indonesian and grew up in Jakarta, where a lot of this started. ACE began after I saw how air pollution and unsafe infrastructure shaped daily life there. YUM Farm is where I fell in love with service.

I speak English and Indonesian, and a little Mandarin.

Outside of work I boulder and watch too many movies. Both require more patience than people expect.

The app I can’t live without is Readwise. It quietly saves everything I highlight, from articles and papers to random 2 a.m. reads, and resurfaces it later so the ideas actually stick.
