A note from the director

Hi, I’m Sheina.

I’m a junior at UC Berkeley studying Data Science and Business at Haas, and my work sits at the intersection of AI, product, and strategy.

This past summer that meant building an agentic procurement platform at Cloudflare. Before that it meant consulting for Series B through F companies on AI product decisions, launching Perplexity on campus (2,000 signups in 24 hours), and supporting M&A deals in Indonesia at Deloitte (market sizing, buyer segmentation, due diligence and financial models). In high school I founded a cement company that rebuilt earthquake-damaged infrastructure and ended up on CNN. I’m still not entirely sure how that happened.

What connects all of it is a tendency to show up at the messy early stage of hard problems, figure out what is actually going on, and build something that makes it better. I care about working on things that are genuinely difficult, with people who take the work seriously.

Lately I’ve been doing more of the building myself as I move toward AI/ML. This little picture house is where I keep what I make along the way. The first two started with things that bugged me: the air I grew up breathing, and how many cookies follow me around the internet.

Warmly,Sheina

P.S. If you also boulder or have a movie I need to watch, tell me. Both require more patience than people expect.

Feature No. 1Live data

Air Quality Explorer

  • web app
  • 2026
  • data viz
  • forecasting
  • maps

Live PM2.5 for 25 cities, mostly around the Bay plus big U.S. cities and Jakarta. Click a city for a week of history, a five-day forecast, a plain-English health note, and a small model that picks the cleanest hour to head outside. It grew out of my PM2.5 research at the Energy Institute at Haas.

A note on the print: data from a global air-quality model, so it shows regional air, not street-level sensors.

Feature No. 2v1.0.0

Cookie Lens

  • chrome extension
  • 2026
  • privacy
  • interface design

A Chrome extension that shows every cookie on the page you’re on and labels the trackers with a plain reason, like “set by pubmatic.com, a known ad tracker, lasts 3 months.” Delete them one at a time or all at once, and open the dashboard to see which companies follow you across the most sites.

A note on the print: ratings come from an open cookie database and Disconnect’s tracker list. Nothing leaves your browser.

Proudest momenttop 3 / 25 finalists / 500+ applicants

Top 3 in the $50K BU Crypto Sustainability Competition

I was especially proud to be named a Top 3 finalist in Boston University’s $50K Sustainability Competition, where we were selected as the representative team for UC Berkeley. The prompt focused on crypto-based solutions, which was unexpected, and our team was competing against PhD and MBA students from around the world. Despite this, we advanced to the final round and placed in the top three out of more than 500 applicants and 25 finalist teams. The experience was incredibly intense; we learned and built rapidly within 24 hours, and it was one of the most rewarding learning experiences I’ve had.
